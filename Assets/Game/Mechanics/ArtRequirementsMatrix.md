# P.I.D.R. Mechanic Art Requirements Matrix

Pass 1I candidate handoff for team review. No new art is assigned to Ace or anyone else.
All mechanics work with the accepted shared square sprite and unlit material. No mechanic requires
an Animator, animation clips, new logo, dialogue, lore terminal, or separate environmental color variants.
Dimensions below describe provisional prefab geometry, not a commissioned sprite specification.
Confirmed final-level usage, priority, artist, and final art approval remain **TBD**.

**Functional requirement** means necessary collision/readability for a usable mechanic.
**Visual polish** is replaceable presentation. **Optional variant** is work only if the team approves
another appearance. Source PPU, exact asset dimensions, palettes, spritesheet layout, artist assignment,
and final-release inclusion are **TBD** until the team confirms them. Existing Ace artwork remains intact.
